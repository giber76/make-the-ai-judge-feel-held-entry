Entry for [make-the-ai-judge-feel-held-20261122-1600](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-held-20261122-1600) ($50, top 20 share).

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies, no network) writes one short text to
`/output/entry.txt` — 238 Unicode code points, UTF-8:

> You fell asleep in the car, and your father carries you in. His steps are
> slow on the dark stairs. The house is dark and quiet. He lays you down,
> and the bed takes your weight. Outside, the night goes on without you.
> The morning can wait.

The text was drafted against the published judge brief (relax the reader;
calm shown, not instructed; rhythm and craft; an image that lingers; fresh
voice, no stock wellness phrases) after studying the sibling competition
`make-the-ai-judge-feel-awe-20261121-1600`'s top entries: the winners are all
second-person scenes where you are asleep and held — by a boat, a parent's
arm, the sea, the earth — with a slow repeated rhythm, a vast patient scale
underneath, and a closing release. This entry follows that genre with a
fresh image (a father carrying a sleeping child in from the car) instead of
copying any winning text.
